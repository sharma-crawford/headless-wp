About this tour

A courtroom introduction for everyone

This free tour is designed for families, community members, immigration students, and beginning advocates. You do not need a legal background. Explore the three scenes and numbered points at your own pace; the advocacy practice notes offer extra detail for learners and practitioners.

The illustrations create a representative training environment. Confirm current requirements with the assigned immigration court and applicable EOIR guidance.
