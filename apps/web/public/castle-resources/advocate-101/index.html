BEFORE YOU BEGIN · IMMIGRATION 101

Learn from the advocate’s side.

Follow fictional clinic conversations with Maya, Luis, and Sofia while learning the basics of immigration advocacy. This is a self-paced course for students and beginning advocates.

  1. Choose any of the ten lessons from the lesson menu. Read the illustrated story and short explanations.
  2. Select answers and press “Check answer” for feedback. Retry questions as often as you need, then mark the lesson complete when its checks are ready.
  3. Bring the lessons together in the final case file. Your results and progress are saved in this browser.

This course has practice questions, not a household-budget simulator. To explore costs and choices in Elena’s life, visit The Line.

Educational information, not legal advice or authority to represent someone. Do not enter personal or client information.
